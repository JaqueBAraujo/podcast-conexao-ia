# Prompt para criação da vinheta

## Objetivo

Criar uma identidade sonora curta para a abertura e o encerramento do podcast Conexão IA.

## Ferramenta utilizada

ElevenLabs - Sound Effects

## Prompt utilizado

Create an opening audio logo that lasts exactly 5 seconds from beginning to end for a podcast about technology and artificial intelligence.

The sound must develop continuously throughout the full 5 seconds — do not create a short 1–2 second sound followed by silence.

Start with a soft futuristic electronic tone, gradually add subtle digital and synth elements, and build toward an elegant and memorable final note at the end of the fifth second.

The style should be modern, technological, sophisticated, warm and professional, representing innovation, artificial intelligence and connection.

The ending should be clean and polished, allowing the podcast narration to begin immediately after the audio logo.

Exactly 5 seconds of audible sound.
Instrumental only.
No voice, no speech, no vocals.
No silence in the middle.
No aggressive or dramatic sounds.

## Resultado

Foi criada uma vinheta instrumental com identidade tecnológica para ser utilizada na abertura e no encerramento do podcast Conexão IA.

A vinheta foi combinada com a narração do episódio utilizando o editor de áudio online BandLab.
